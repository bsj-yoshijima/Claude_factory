========================================================
自由装飾品（テーマ独自の1点もの）用プロンプト雛形
========================================================
使い方:
  1) 大きさに合う型スクリーンを選んで「編集」
       1x1（正方・小）  4263700531060843034
       1x2（細長・高）  2557222867215896306
       2x2（大きい）    17544647967018932933
  2) 下の【本文】をそのまま貼り、■SUBJECT■ だけ書き換える
  3) 取り込み:
       node tools/assets/cut_prop_sheet.mjs <画像> --single --name <theme>_<id>
     はみ出して切れる場合だけ --scale 0.9 や --pad 4 で救済する（家具7点では使わない）
注意:
  - 全角ダッシュ（—）を入れると Stitch がエラーを返す
  - シアンは「トンボ」であって背景ではない。ここを一番強く言わないと必ず塗り潰される

【本文】ここから
--------------------------------------------------------
REPAINT THIS EXACT SHELL. Output the same image size as the reference.

READ THESE THREE RULES FIRST. If any of them is broken the image is rejected.

RULE A. THE CYAN RECTANGLE MUST STAY ONE UNBROKEN CLOSED LOOP of thin cyan (#00E5FF) line, exactly where it is in the reference. No part of the object may touch it, cross it or hide it.

RULE B. THE CYAN DIAMOND ON THE FLOOR IS A THIN OUTLINE AND IT STAYS A THIN OUTLINE. Do NOT fill it. Do NOT paint a cyan mat, a cyan platform, cyan sand, cyan water or a cyan shadow under the object. There is NO cyan surface anywhere in the picture, only the two thin lines. Never use cyan, turquoise, aqua or sea-blue on the object itself; use navy blue when you need blue.

RULE C. THE OBJECT IS SMALL. Draw it at about 70 percent of the frame width and about 70 percent of the frame height (for the tall 1x2 frame: 60 percent wide and 80 percent tall), so there is a WIDE band of pure magenta between the object and the cyan rectangle on the left, on the right and above. Only the bottom is tight: the lowest pixel of the object sits ON the bottom cyan line, centred left to right, with no magenta gap under it.

Its FOOTPRINT matches the cyan diamond on the floor: not wider, not deeper.

THE CAMERA IS FIXED AND IT IS NOT A FRONT VIEW. This is a game object seen from an overhead isometric camera.
- True 2:1 isometric: every horizontal edge on the ground travels 2 pixels across for exactly 1 pixel down.
- Exactly TWO vertical faces of any box are visible, one facing lower-left and one facing lower-right. Never one flat face facing the viewer.
- YOU MUST SEE THE TOP FACES: the top of the base, the top of every upright, the top of the whole object.

BACKGROUND: solid pure MAGENTA (#FF00FF) everywhere around the object. NO floor, NO ground plane, NO cast shadow, NO border, NO frame of your own, NO grid, NO text, NO letters, NO numbers.

■SUBJECT■ ここから（作るものだけ書き換える）
THE OBJECT: <何か。例: a LIFEGUARD TOWER: a small square hut of sun-bleached planks with a coral-red and cream striped canvas roof, an open window on the lower-left face, standing on four short driftwood legs with a ladder against the lower-right face>.
Materials: <素材。例: sun-bleached driftwood, weathered planks, striped canvas, rope, woven straw>.
Palette: <5色から6色。例: warm pale sand, driftwood grey, coral red, sun yellow, navy blue, foam white>.
■SUBJECT■ ここまで

STYLE: VERY chunky lo-fi 8-bit Famicom pixel art, extreme high contrast, thick clean black outlines, flat colors, no gradients, no anti-aliasing. It is shown about 50 to 100 pixels tall in the game, so keep it BOLD: few large shapes, a strong silhouette.
